Moments from Lesotho

Lesotho Mountains

Sunlit ridges and grassy slopes of the Maloti, the highland kingdom's mountains.

Mountain Landscapes

Jagged peaks rise above green valleys, showing the dramatic terrain that earned Lesotho the name "Kingdom in the Sky".

Afriski

A snow-covered road winds through the winter landscape around Afriski Mountain Resort, one of the few places in southern Africa where you can ski.

Katse Dam Wall

The towering arch wall of Katse Dam, a centerpiece of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project that supplies water to Lesotho and South Africa.

Maletsunyane Falls

Basotho horse riders pass the Maletsunyane Falls near Semonkong, where the water drops about 192 metres into a sandstone gorge.

Thaba Bosiu

The historic mountain stronghold of King Moshoeshoe I, the birthplace of the Basotho nation, overlooking the plains below.

What Makes Lesotho Special

01

Kingdom in the Sky

The entire country sits above 1,000 metres, with the lowest point higher than any other country's lowest point. Its highlands earn it the nickname.

02

Basotho Culture

Famous for the cone-shaped Basotho hat and the colourful Basotho blanket, worn daily and on ceremonial occasions as symbols of identity.

03

Mountain Terrain

Rugged peaks, deep valleys, and sweeping plateaus make Lesotho a paradise for hikers, trekkers, and photographers.

04

Outdoor Adventures

Enjoy pony trekking, hiking, mountain biking, and even skiing at Afriski during the winter months.

05

Rich History

Founded by King Moshoeshoe I in the 19th century, Lesotho gained independence from Britain in 1966 and remains a constitutional monarchy.

06

Waterfalls & Rivers

Home to Maletsunyane Falls, one of southern Africa's highest single-drop waterfalls, and the source of rivers that supply the region.
